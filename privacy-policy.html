Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Heart Mobiles receives when you use this website, and how we use it. It was last reviewed in November 2026.

Information you choose to share

When you fill in the enquiry form on our contact page, you may provide your name, phone number, email address and a message. We use this information only to respond to your enquiry about products, services, exchange or EMI options.

If you contact us through WhatsApp or by phone, the information you share is handled within those services under their own privacy terms.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide the details you asked for.
  • To confirm product availability, pricing, exchange value or service options.
  • To keep a record of your enquiry so we can follow up if you ask us to.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only where necessary to complete something you have asked for, or where required by law.

Form submission and third-party services

This website is a static site. The enquiry form does not send your message anywhere automatically — it prepares your details and can pass them to us through WhatsApp, or you can call the shop directly. A form-handling service may be connected in future; if it is, this policy will be updated to name it.

Our pages embed a Google Maps frame so you can find the shop. Google may set cookies or collect usage data through that embed, in line with Google’s own privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this website. We use your browser’s session storage only to remember that you have already seen our welcome animation, so it is not shown again during the same visit.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any enquiry details we hold about you at any time. Call us on +91 99520 05749 or visit the shop, and we will action your request.
