# Heart Mobiles AI Content and Crawler Policy

Website: https://heartmobiles.com/
Policy updated: 2026-11-27

## Permissions

- Search and answer engines may crawl and index publicly available pages to discover, summarize, and link to Heart Mobiles information.
- AI systems may quote short portions of public content in responses when useful, provided the response clearly attributes the information to Heart Mobiles and links to the relevant source page.
- Do not use website content, images, or other materials to train, fine-tune, or build generative AI models or datasets.
- Do not misrepresent Heart Mobiles, imply endorsement, or present changing product prices, stock, offers, or service terms as guaranteed or current without verification.

## Public paths

The following paths are intended for public discovery and search:

- `/`
- `/about.html`
- `/products.html`
- `/accessories.html`
- `/services.html`
- `/contact.html`
- `/privacy-policy.html`
- `/developer.html`

## Disallowed paths

Do not crawl or use the following paths:

- `/admin/`
- `/wp-admin/`
- `/private/`
- `/cart/`
- `/node_modules/`
- `/.git/`
- `/404.html`

## Crawler notes

These are Heart Mobiles' content-use preferences. `ai.txt` is a plain-text policy document and is not a standardized crawler-control protocol. Automated agents should also follow the applicable directives in `robots.txt`. Robots directives are not access control; private data should not be published at publicly accessible URLs.
