About Heart Mobiles

Heart Mobiles is a family-run mobile showroom in New Pet, Krishnagiri. For over a decade we have helped customers choose the right phone, keep it protected, and get it fixed quickly when something goes wrong.

Our story

Built on relationships, not just sales

Heart Mobiles began with a simple idea: make buying and maintaining a phone straightforward for people in Krishnagiri. Instead of pushing the most expensive handset, we ask how you use your phone, what your budget is and what matters most to you.

That approach has kept customers coming back — and bringing their families and friends along. Today the shop stocks leading brands, keeps a full wall of accessories, and handles hardware and software service in-house.
